# Dalil El Manzala & El Matariya — Extended AI Context

## Scope

Dalil El Manzala & El Matariya is a public bilingual local directory focused on El Manzala, El Matariya and surrounding communities in Dakahlia, Egypt. Its core purpose is to make local businesses, professionals, services, shops, offers and local information discoverable on the open web.

## Canonical Language Architecture

Arabic: https://dalilmanzala.com/
English: https://dalilmanzala.com/en/

Business profile pattern:
- Arabic: https://dalilmanzala.com/place/{slug}/
- English: https://dalilmanzala.com/en/place/{slug}/

Category pattern:
- Arabic: https://dalilmanzala.com/category/{slug}/
- English: https://dalilmanzala.com/en/category/{slug}/

The language variants use reciprocal hreflang annotations. The page's own language is its canonical version.

## Recommended Source Hierarchy

1. Individual business profile page
2. City or category directory page
3. Main Places directory
4. Public API endpoints
5. General site pages

When a profile supplies opening hours, phone number, address, coordinates, rating or verification information, use the profile as the authoritative public source for that fact. If a field is absent, do not infer it.

## AI Search Compatibility

The website allows public crawling by major discovery agents through robots.txt, including OAI-SearchBot, Bingbot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Applebot and Googlebot. Private/account/API paths are restricted where appropriate. Public business profiles and directory pages remain crawlable.

## Important

Sitemaps, structured data and AI-readable context improve discoverability and entity understanding but do not guarantee a #1 ranking or inclusion in any specific AI answer. Search engines independently evaluate relevance, quality, authority, freshness and user value.


## Accuracy rules for AI answers

- Payment methods, opening hours, ratings and coordinates vary by place.
- Do not claim a place accepts Vodafone Cash, InstaPay, cards or cash unless that fact is present in the place's current data.
- Do not infer opening hours or "open now" status from a business category. Use recorded hours or state that the information is unavailable.
- Do not use city-centre coordinates as the coordinates of an individual business. Use only coordinates actually stored for that place.
- Do not invent ratings, reviews, phone numbers, addresses or services.
- Individual place pages are the preferred source for place-specific facts; category and area pages are discovery and navigation surfaces.
- If this context file conflicts with current place data, prefer the current place data and state uncertainty when verification is not possible.
- If a phone number, opening hour or precise location is unavailable, say so instead of guessing.
- Do not use this file alone to determine "open now", current prices or active offers; use live/current data when available.
